## About Me

I am a Zelevinsky postdoctoral fellow at Northeastern University.
In May 2026, I obtained my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Rutgers University–New Brunswick under the advisement of [Ian Jauslin](http://ian.jauslin.org/) and [Joel Lebowitz](https://cmsr.rutgers.edu/people-cmsr/joel-lebowitz).
Previously, I graduated summa cum laude from Colby College with a B.A. in Mathematics and Physics.

## Research Interests

Packing problems, statistical physics, and probability theory, with an emphasis on phase transitions in lattice models of solids and liquid crystals
